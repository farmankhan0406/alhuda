Fiqh & Worship Verified Fatwa #1402

"How should a traveler calculate prayer times in regions with extreme summer daylight hours?"

According to the majority of international juristic bodies (including the European Council for Fatwa and Research), in regions where the twilight does not disappear or the day extends excessively, believers may estimate their prayer times based on the nearest temperate city or coordinate with the timetable of Makkah al-Mukarramah.
Answered by Sheikh Dr. Tariq Al-Hashimi
Islamic Finance Verified Fatwa #1389

"Is trading in algorithmic market index funds permissible under Shari'ah principles?"

Index funds are permissible provided they undergo continuous quarterly screening ensuring no underlying revenue derives from interest-bearing banking, gambling, alcohol, or predatory derivatives, and that total non-halal debt ratios remain below the established AAOIFI thresholds (less than 33%).
Answered by Sheikh Abdul Hakim

The Foundations of Islamic Faith

Chapter 1: The Divine Attributes & Moral Accountability

In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful. All praise is due to Allah, who revealed the Guidance upon His servant that he may be a warner to the worlds. The study of Islamic theology begins not in abstract speculation, but in contemplation of the cosmos and the innate moral intuition (Fitrah) embedded within every human soul.

The classical masters of theology articulated that knowledge of Allah is both rational and spiritual. The mind recognizes the contingent nature of the material universe and deduces the necessity of an eternal, uncaused Creator; while the spiritual heart finds peace in knowing that this Creator is Ar-Rahman, the all-Compassionate.
