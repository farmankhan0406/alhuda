Fiqh & Worship Verified Fatwa #1402

"How should a traveler calculate prayer times in regions with extreme summer daylight hours?"

According to the majority of international juristic bodies and the Islamic Fiqh Academy, in regions where twilight does not disappear or the day extends excessively, believers may estimate their prayer times based on the nearest temperate city or coordinate with the timetable of Makkah al-Mukarramah.
Verified by Sheikh Hafiz Saleem Sanabili Madani
Islamic Finance Verified Fatwa #1389

"Is trading in algorithmic market index funds permissible under Shari'ah principles?"

Index funds are permissible provided they undergo rigorous quarterly screening ensuring no underlying revenue derives from interest-bearing banking, gambling, alcohol, or speculative derivatives, and that total interest-bearing debt ratios remain below the established AAOIFI Shari'ah thresholds (less than 33%).
Verified by AL-HUDA Islamic Jurisprudence Council
Family & Marriage Verified Fatwa #1365

"What is the Shari'ah ruling regarding writing conditions into the Islamic marriage contract (Nikah)?"

Conditions that do not violate the core objectives of marriage or contradict divine prohibitions are valid and binding upon the husband, in accordance with the Prophet’s ﷺ hadith: 'The conditions most deserving of fulfillment are those by which intimacy is made lawful for you' [Sahih al-Bukhari].
Verified by Sheikh Hafiz Saleem Sanabili Madani
Aqeedah & Belief Verified Fatwa #1342

"How does one overcome persistent whisperings (Waswas) in faith and prayer?"

Seek refuge in Allah from the accursed Shaytan, recite Surah Al-Ikhlas and the Mu'awwidhatayn, ignore obsessive repetition, and remember that hating and struggling against doubts is a sign of clear, genuine Iman, as affirmed by the Messenger of Allah ﷺ.
Verified by Sheikh Hafiz Saleem Sanabili Madani

The Foundations of Islamic Faith

Chapter 1: The Divine Attributes & Moral Accountability

In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful. All praise is due to Allah, who revealed the Guidance upon His servant that he may be a warner to the worlds. The study of Islamic theology begins not in abstract speculation, but in contemplation of the cosmos and the innate moral intuition (Fitrah) embedded within every human soul.

The classical masters of theology articulated that knowledge of Allah is both rational and spiritual. The mind recognizes the contingent nature of the material universe and deduces the necessity of an eternal, uncaused Creator; while the spiritual heart finds peace in knowing that this Creator is Ar-Rahman, the all-Compassionate.
